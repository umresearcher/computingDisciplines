# Ounces of Computing

Making discipline-relevant computing and AI accessible to every student in every discipline and at every institution of higher education.

Ounces of computing are small, discipline-aligned, course-embedded learning experiences that integrate computing directly into existing courses while advancing disciplinary learning goals.

Copyright © 2024 The Regents of the University of Michigan

## Key Features of an Ounce of Computing

- Defined by the learning outcomes of the discipline course in which it appears.
- Aligned with the technological, user-oriented, and/or sociocultural perspectives of computing, depending on disciplinary needs.
- Includes programming only when programming supports the disciplinary learning goals.
- Co-designed by disciplinary instructors and computing experts while respecting disciplinary epistemologies.
- Applicable across research universities, regional public universities, liberal-arts colleges, and community colleges.

## Evaluating an Ounce of Computing

An ounce of computing should be:

- Effective in helping students achieve the intended learning outcomes.
- Useful for advancing disciplinary learning goals.
- Usable by students with varied backgrounds and preparation.
- Accessible to the intended student population.
- Sustainable for disciplinary instructors to integrate and maintain.
- Supportive of the demographic diversity of the discipline without reproducing barriers often associated with traditional computing pathways.

Computing serves disciplinary learning goals. The disciplinary learning goals remain paramount.

## Projects

### Discrete Math

Interactive materials that connect discrete mathematics concepts such as logic, relations, graphs, matrices, databases, and inference to computing.

🌐 https://discretemath-umflint.streamlit.app/

---

### Health Sciences

Interactive epidemiology tools designed to reduce barriers created by mathematical calculations and math anxiety. Students focus on understanding epidemiological concepts and interpretation rather than computation mechanics.

🌐 https://umresearcher.github.io/computingDisciplines/HealthSciences/calculators.html

---

### Market Basket Analysis

Interactive materials that help marketing and business students understand market basket analysis, association rules, support, confidence, and related measures through exploration of transactional data.

🌐 https://marketbasketanalysis-umflint.streamlit.app/
